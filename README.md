# The Ostrich Challenge

A self-contained survival challenge mod for **7 Days to Die V3.3 and V3.4**.

You are the night security guard at the Ostrich. Every room above the ground floor holds sleeping guests, and more arrive all night to take any room you have emptied. Your job is to empty the hotel completely, and then deal with the thing in the basement.

It cannot be killed while the hotel has guests in it. Shoot it, blow it up, drop it down a stairwell, and it gets back up. It is tied to the hotel being open, and the hotel closes when it is empty.

**Death is permanent. If it gets you, your run is over.**

---

## The job

Guests arrive from ten in the morning through to four the next morning, three quarters of them after dark. A room counts as empty once you have cleared it, and an empty room is immediately available to the next guest who walks through the door.

So clearing rooms is not progress on its own. Progress is clearing them faster than the hotel can fill them.

Four things have to reach nothing at the same time:

- guests asleep in rooms
- guests already walking the building
- guests stood in reception with nowhere to go
- guests still on their way in

When the hotel reads zero you can shut the doors, and for the next twenty four hours the thing in the basement can be killed.

---

## What is in it

**The hotel.** Fourteen guest floors, a ground floor, a basement and a roof, every room of it occupied.

**The check-in system.** Guests arrive at the doors on a schedule, report to reception, and take the rooms you clear. If there is nowhere to put them they wait in the lobby, and if the lobby fills the hotel turns them away.

**The stalker.** It hunts by sound. Your footsteps carry a few floors and a shotgun is heard across the whole building. It hears through floors and ceilings. What it gets is a guess at where you were rather than where you are, and the gap between the two is most of what keeps you alive. Crouching silences your feet. It does not silence your gun.

**Six difficulty tiers**, chosen on the New Game screen: Night Porter, Duty Manager, Full House, No Vacancies, Do Not Disturb and The Last Guest. Full House is the default. On the four easier tiers the stalker takes blood moon night off. On the two hardest it works it.

**Two terminals.** The laptop in the roof portacabin reads the register as it stood at five this morning, by floor. The terminal in the front office reads the hotel live, and it is on the ground floor, which is the stalker's own part of the building.

**A ham radio** on the roof, and a man on the other end who talks to you once per game day. He knows the hotel perfectly well. He has no idea what is happening inside it.

**A How to play page** on the ESC menu, so no player has to guess what they are supposed to be doing.

**An ending.** Put the stalker down inside the window, take the key fob out of the bag it drops, and drive out of the basement car park.

## Installing

1. Disable **Easy Anti-Cheat**. This mod uses Harmony and will not run with EAC on.
2. Drop the `OstrichChallenge` folder into your `Mods` folder.
3. Start a new game and pick one of the six Ostrich presets on the New Game screen.

Set your sandbox options before you start.

---

## Compatibility

Built and tested against **V3.3.0 (b18)** and **V3.4.0 (b6)**.

A world started on 3.3 loads on 3.4.
